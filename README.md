🧭 WPI MA529 – Stochastic Processes for Generative AI

Graduate coursework repository for MA529: Stochastic Processes for Generative AI at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI). This course bridges rigorous probability theory with modern generative modeling, providing the mathematical foundations behind diffusion models, variational inference, and score-based generation.

⚙️ Course Focus

Probability theory, stochastic processes, diffusion models, Markov chains, and mathematical foundations of generative AI.

Key Topics & Projects
Markov Chains & Convergence - Analysis of discrete and continuous-time Markov chains, mixing times, and stationary distributions — the theoretical backbone of MCMC-based sampling in generative models.
Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs) - Implementation and simulation of Itô and Stratonovich SDEs; Euler–Maruyama and Milstein integrators used in score-based diffusion frameworks (DDPM, score matching).
Score-Based Generative Modeling - Explored the connection between Langevin dynamics and denoising diffusion: deriving score functions, training noise-conditional networks, and running reverse-time SDEs for image synthesis.
Variational Inference & ELBO - Derived the evidence lower bound (ELBO), implemented reparameterization tricks, and connected VAE objectives to the broader theory of KL divergence minimization.
Monte Carlo Methods - Metropolis–Hastings, Gibbs sampling, and importance sampling — applied to Bayesian posterior estimation and normalizing constant approximation.
